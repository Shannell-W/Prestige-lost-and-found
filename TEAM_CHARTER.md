## 2. Existing App Summary

The Campus Lost and Found website is a student-focused platform designed to help students recover lost belongings within their school community. Students can register and log in, access their school-specific portal, browse and search for found items, report items they have found, and submit ownership claims with supporting evidence. Students who find items can review claims and select the rightful owner, while users can also manage their claims, view their posted items, receive notifications, access their profiles, and use features such as Dark Mode.

## 3. Part B Audit

### Information Stored

- Student ID
- Notifications ID
- Items ID
- School and school-portal information
- Found item details
- Item photos/evidence
- Ownership claims
- Claim evidence and claim status
- Notifications
- Users' posted items
- User profile information
- Authentication/session information

### User Actions

- Register
- Log in
- Enter their school portal
- Browse found items
- Search for items
- Report found items
- Submit ownership claims
- Provide evidence for claims
- View their claims
- View items they have posted
- Review claims on items they found
- Select the legitimate owner
- View notifications
- View their profile
- Toggle Dark Mode
- Log out
